# Sky Glass Portfolio

Design a mobile-first, single-page personal portfolio website utilizing a pristine  "Glassmorphism" UI design style. The strict color palette must consist ONLY of Sky Blue and White. The background should be a soft, gradient sky blue. All elements should feature soft, puffy 3D clay-like drop shadows and frosted white glass blur effects (glassmorphism) with white borders to create a tactile, floating appearance.

Layout & Elements (Top to Bottom):

Header: Top left says "Good morning," in white text, with a large bold white greeting "Alex" underneath. Remove any hamburger menu. Top right corner must have a circular frosted white glass button with sky blue text labeled "About".

Hero Card (Glassmorphism): A large, frosted white glass container floating in the center with soft clay shadows. Inside this card, place a prominent "Today" date widget styled in white with sky blue accents.

Quick Actions: Below the date widget, a row of four circular, claymorphic buttons (white with soft sky blue 3D shadows). Include icons and labels for: WhatsApp, Instagram, Email, and Telegram.

Your Projects Section: A section titled "Your projects" in white text. On the right side of this title, add a clickable text link "See all" (this link should navigate to a separate, dedicated Projects page). List three pill-shaped, claymorphic project cards (white with soft sky blue 3D shadows):

"Mobile App Prototype" (Sky blue icon)

"Marketing Website" (Sky blue icon)

"Design System" (Sky blue icon)

Footer Navigation Bar: A fixed bottom glassmorphic floating dock (frosted white glass). It must contain exactly four icons with labels: Home, Search, Hire me, and Profile. Icons should be sky blue.

Vibe: Clean, modern, airy, tactile, iOS-inspired layout, incredibly polished, soft shadows, exclusively sky blue and white.
